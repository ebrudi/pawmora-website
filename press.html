About the game

Pawmora is a cozy game project about customizing an adorable animal companion, decorating a home and exploring a blossom-filled village. Fashion, fishing and seasonal activities are part of its development vision.

Developer

Ebru Dinç · Independent

Engine

Unity 6 URP

Status

Development preview; release date unannounced.

Platforms

iOS is targeted; a Windows development preview exists. Other platforms are unconfirmed.

Meet Mochi

With cream-colored fur, purple eyes and fairytale fashion, Mochi embodies Pawmora's character vision. The in-game model and animations are in development.
